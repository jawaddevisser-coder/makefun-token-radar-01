How the value is worked out

The multiple is the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the live protocols in the same category that report both numbers (the global median when a category has fewer than three). Growth is the seven-day TVL change, clamped to −25%…+80%. Maturity rises with days since listing, up to three years. Breadth adds 3% per extra chain, up to ten. Live protocols are not modelled: their implied value is their market cap, the market’s own number, shown so a reader can compare a pre-token protocol against what already trades.

Not ranked as launch candidates

These protocols record no token, but the house’s own rule identifies them as a wrapped, staked or bridged product of a token that already trades, a versioned name, or an exchange product. Farming them for a token that will never exist would be bad advice, so they carry no implied value and no farm plan — they are listed here, with the reason, instead of being silently dropped.
